Venad-style Ashok Leyland KSRTC

The first detailed vehicle pass is based on the supplied reference photograph: off-white body, sky-blue lower livery, single-piece windscreen, traditional front grille and lamps, side glazing, passenger doors, wheel arches and a visible passenger cabin. The road and route are still prototype scenery.

Development note

This is a high-detail procedural 3D blockout, not a finished factory-accurate model. Next we will refine the body geometry, authentic lettering and cabin details, then build the researched Thrissur–Palakkad route.
