PHASE 02 • VEHICLE REBUILD

Venad-style Ashok Leyland KSRTC

The vehicle is being refined against the supplied Venad Ashok Leyland reference photograph: tall high-floor body, single-piece windscreen, one driver's-side wiper, traditional ribbed front grille, round lower headlamps, blue lower band, dark side windows and two-door layout. The road and route remain prototype scenery.

Development note

This is a high-detail procedural 3D blockout, not a finished factory-accurate model. Next we will refine the body geometry, authentic lettering and cabin details, then build the researched Thrissur–Palakkad route.
